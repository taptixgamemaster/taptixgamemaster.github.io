A little help
with your breeze.

Pair a compatible fan, test the controls, and keep everything nearby.

Start with compatibility

Breeze controls selected ceiling-fan receivers that use FanLamp Pro or LampSmart Pro Bluetooth protocols. It includes FanLamp v1, v2, and v3 and LampSmart v1 and v3 profiles. Your receiver determines which controls work.

Check the app named in your fan's manual. A matching brand, product photo, or Bluetooth label alone does not establish compatibility. Test your actual fan before buying the lifetime unlock.

Breeze sends commands directly from your iPhone while the app is open. Fan control does not require a Mac, hub, Wi-Fi connection, or developer account. Internet access may be needed to load a purchase price, buy, or restore through Apple.

Pair your fan

  1. Open Set up your fan. If the manual names FanLamp Pro, start with FanLamp Pro v3. Choose a LampSmart profile only for a receiver that uses LampSmart Pro.
  2. Tap Enable Bluetooth controls, allow access, and keep your iPhone near the fan.
  3. Follow the fan manual's pairing instructions. A common procedure is to turn wall power off for about 10 seconds, turn it on, then tap Pair now within five seconds. Keep other compatible fans off during pairing.
  4. Watch for a flash or listen for a beep. Then test Light on and Light off. Confirm I saw my fan respond only after you observe a control work.
  5. If it does not respond, try the next profile for the same protocol family and repeat the power-cycle and pairing steps. If no profile works, the receiver may be unsupported.

Do not pair through iPhone Bluetooth Settings. Keep Breeze open and the phone unlocked while sending, and leave the fan's wall power on during normal use.

Your trial and lifetime unlock

You get 10 free control commands. A command counts once its Bluetooth broadcast completes successfully, even if the fan does not respond. The app cannot read a fan-state acknowledgement.

Pairing, fan off, light off, and turning everything off remain free. A failed or interrupted broadcast does not consume a trial command. Trial usage stays locally on the iPhone, and reinstalling does not necessarily reset it.

The optional lifetime unlock is one nonconsumable in-app purchase. There is no subscription, automatic conversion, or automatic charge at the end of the trial. Apple's purchase sheet displays your local price before you confirm.

The unlock removes the command limit. It cannot make an incompatible receiver work. The purchase button becomes available after you confirm that a control worked on your fan.

Restore a purchase

Open the trial or unlimited-controls card and tap Restore purchase using the same Apple Account that bought the unlock. An internet connection may be required. Pairing may need to be repeated on a new iPhone.

Refunds

Apple manages App Store payments and refund requests. Use Apple's purchase support if you need to request a refund. A revoked or refunded purchase removes paid access; pairing and off controls remain free.

If something does not work

Bluetooth controls are unavailable

Turn Bluetooth on, allow Breeze access in iPhone Settings, and return to the app. Keep the phone unlocked and Breeze in the foreground while sending.

The app says it sent a command, but nothing changed

A completed broadcast confirms that iOS accepted transmission. It does not confirm reception by your fan. Move closer, check wall power, and repeat setup with a matching profile. Do not buy the unlock to troubleshoot compatibility.

All 10 trial commands are used

You can still pair and use off controls. If the light is visibly on, try Light off to check a real response. Only confirm compatibility after observing the fan respond.

The price is unavailable or a purchase is pending

Check your internet connection and tap Refresh price. A pending Apple purchase or Ask to Buy request unlocks controls only when approved. Use Restore purchase for an existing completed purchase.

A local storage error appears

Unlock your iPhone and reopen the app. The app will not reset an unreadable trial counter or silently assume that a purchase exists. If an error occurred after transmission began, check the fan before trying the command again.
