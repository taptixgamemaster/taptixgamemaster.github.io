Privacy policy · Last updated October 16, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains the Breeze iPhone app, this support website, and information you choose to provide when asking for help. “We” refers to Mezherium Enterprises LLC, the publisher of Breeze. The app has no developer account, advertising, analytics, tracking, third-party purchase service, or automatic diagnostic-upload service.

Information stored on your iPhone

The app keeps the information needed to act as your remote:

  • A randomly generated remote identifier and protocol command counter.
  • Your chosen fan protocol, setup preference, and whether you confirmed a physical response.
  • The number of free trial commands you have used.

These values are stored in the iPhone's device-only Keychain without iCloud synchronization. We do not receive them or use them to identify you. Keychain items may remain after uninstalling, so reinstalling does not necessarily reset pairing details or the trial. Device-only items do not migrate to another iPhone; pairing and setup may need to be repeated there.

The app also displays recent requested controls while it is running. These are requests, not readings obtained from the fan. There is no developer-hosted history of your controls.

Bluetooth stays nearby

When you send a control, the app broadcasts the remote identifier and protocol command by Bluetooth to nearby compatible receivers. These radio broadcasts are not sent to us or to an internet service. Nearby devices may receive Bluetooth broadcasts.

Breeze does not scan for or upload a list of nearby devices and does not read a fan-state acknowledgement. It requests Bluetooth access when you enable controls. It does not request your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Apple handles purchases

The optional lifetime unlock uses Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes your payment and supplies signed purchase and entitlement information, which the app verifies on your device. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password, and the app does not send purchase transactions to a developer server.

Apple may process purchase, fraud-prevention, diagnostic, or usage information under its own policies and the settings you choose. This policy does not say that Apple collects no information. See Apple's Privacy Policy and your Apple privacy settings.

If you contact support

There is no support form, ticket submission, or automatic log attachment inside Breeze. If you choose to contact us by email outside the app, we receive the email address and message contents you send. We use them to answer and resolve your request, not for advertising or tracking.

Provide only the details you want to share. Do not send passwords or payment information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. You can ask us to delete your correspondence using the contact below; any required retention will be explained. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete your iPhone's Keychain items.

This website

These support pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, embedded media, or external font and image requests. We do not add cookies or website analytics. External links open the linked provider's website when you select them.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not visitors are signed into GitHub. GitHub may also process routine request and device information under its privacy statement. We do not maintain a separate visitor database.

Hosting-related processing and retention follow GitHub's practices. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This website disclosure is separate from the app, which sends no app data to a developer server.

Sharing and choices

We do not sell app data or share it for advertising. We have no app account or developer database to export or delete. You can stop Bluetooth use by revoking the app's Bluetooth permission in iPhone Settings. Deleting the app stops its operation but may leave the device-only Keychain entries described above.

Apple manages your purchase history and refund requests. Restoring a lifetime unlock on another iPhone uses the Apple Account that purchased it; it does not transfer your local trial or fan setup data to us.

Changes to this policy

If the app or support practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Any future collection will be disclosed before it begins and will use any permissions or choices required for that feature.
